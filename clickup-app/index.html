    Frequently asked questions

    How does Vobiz integrate with ClickUp?

    The softphone runs inside your workspace. When you dial or receive calls, your audio connects via WebRTC over Vobiz SIP. Every call detail record (CDR) and recording link is automatically saved to your ClickUp List.

    What is Dual Login?

    You can sign in using your account-wide Vobiz Auth ID & Auth Token to auto-load your phone numbers, or enter your endpoint's SIP username & password for direct SIP registration.

    How do inbound call notifications work?

    When a customer calls your Vobiz DID, you receive an instant incoming call banner with ringtone and desktop notification. When the call concludes, a completion popup confirms the call duration and ClickUp sync.
